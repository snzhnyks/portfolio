01

Seasonal content & DM funnel concept · Panama

Isla Palenque
Private Island Resort

Observed friction

Rare private-island access and wildlife can read as a beautiful but passive gallery.

Green-season rain can feel like a reason to delay, not a reason to travel.

Recommended shift

Make the green season a time-bound reason to book: wildlife, privacy and a mental reset.

Use the DM keyword WHALE to start an availability and experience conversation.

Reel-to-enquiry logic

One guest truth → useful proof → one low-friction next step.

  1. Hook

    “The kind of quiet you only find when the island is yours.”

  2. Value & proof

    A wildlife-and-weather moment makes green season feel intentional, not inconvenient.

  3. Next step

    DM WHALE for seasonal availability and the best stay window.

Before & After:

A proposed feed system for guests planning a private-island escape.

  • Strong imagery lacks one clear booking story.
  • Nature, rooms and food appear without a repeatable rhythm.
  • Seasonality is not framed as a timely reason to travel.
  • Four pillars create a recognisable island narrative.
  • Wildlife, space and local flavour lead each scroll.
  • WHALE turns seasonal attention into a direct enquiry.

02

Off-season growth concept · Boquete, Panama

Finca Lérida
Misty Eco-Luxury Escape

Observed friction

Mist, mountain air and coffee heritage are not always framed as a distinctive premium escape.

Room-led posts can ask for a booking before a high-intent guest feels the value.

Recommended shift

Position rain, Geisha coffee and cool mountain silence as the product — not the low-season compromise.

Use saveable 48-hour guides and the DM keyword GEISHA to move from inspiration to planning.

Reel-to-enquiry logic

One guest truth → useful proof → one low-friction next step.

  1. Hook

    “When the forecast is rain, but the plan is a proper reset.”

  2. Value & proof

    A saveable 48-hour itinerary connects Geisha coffee, birding and mountain quiet.

  3. Next step

    Save the guide or DM GEISHA with travel dates.

Before & After:

A proposed feed structure for a highland reset, coffee discovery and direct planning.

  • Coffee, hotel and events compete in unrelated styles.
  • Promotional moments interrupt the nature-led escape story.
  • No clear series links saveable ideas to stays.
  • Cloud forest, Geisha coffee and birding become repeatable themes.
  • A quieter visual system feels more premium and consistent.
  • Guide-led posts move inspiration toward planning.

03

Profile audit · Content system · Weekly plan

Mana Earthly Paradise
Ubud, Bali

Before recommendations

The mission is visible, but a first-time guest has to work harder to understand stay options and the fastest booking route.

Recommended shift

Put the stay, impact and guest choice in one visible path: bio, Highlights, three pins and a weekly content rhythm.

Use GUIDE as a low-friction DM prompt for first-time planners.

Reel-to-enquiry logic

One guest truth → useful proof → one low-friction next step.

  1. Hook

    “A stay can give something back to the place that holds it.”

  2. Value & proof

    Show one stay choice, one conscious meal and one visible impact detail — one idea at a time.

  3. Next step

    Comment or DM GUIDE for the stay format that fits the trip.

Before & After:

A feed proposal that keeps Mana’s mission central while making its stay easier to choose.

  • Beautiful imagery has no first-time guest journey.
  • Stay, food, culture and impact are visually unstructured.
  • High-intent questions lack recurring answers in the feed.
  • Start here, stay choice and impact become clear content anchors.
  • A warm natural grade unifies villas, people and place.
  • GUIDE-led posts add a low-friction route to planning.

04

Instagram review · Content direction · Guest journey

Suenyo Eco Retreat
Tabanan, Bali

Observed friction

The riverside setting is compelling, yet a new visitor may not immediately know which stay or experience fits their trip.

Wellness, cultural rituals and special occasions need a more deliberate decision path.

Recommended shift

Build a content cadence around Pavilions, Wellness, Culture and Occasions — then support it with practical Highlights and FAQs.

Move from one atmospheric image to a sequence that helps guests identify their reason to stay.

Reel-to-enquiry logic

One guest truth → useful proof → one low-friction next step.

  1. Hook

    “Bali feels different when the river sets the pace.”

  2. Value & proof

    A ritual, pavilion detail or team gesture turns atmosphere into a tangible reason to stay.

  3. Next step

    DM RIVER with dates and the occasion for a tailored suggestion.

Before & After:

A proposed feed direction from the retreat’s calm visual world to a clear reason to enquire.

  • Mood-led content does not distinguish each reason to stay.
  • Wellness, food, river and interiors compete for attention.
  • The feed has few clear, saveable planning formats.
  • Pavilions, wellness and rituals become identifiable series.
  • A green-and-warm palette makes the retreat recognisable.
  • Practical hooks support direct enquiries beside atmosphere.
